About Portfolio Builder

Portfolio Builder is a portfolio modelling and fund research tool for regulated financial brokers and advisers in Ireland. It models how a combination of funds would have performed, and it illustrates possible outcomes. It does not recommend anything.

What it does

  • Builds a portfolio from up to five funds and shows its past performance, volatility and largest fall.
  • Compares two portfolios side by side over the same period.
  • Researches individual funds: returns over standard periods, charges against the standard fee, and what each fund holds.
  • Produces a print document and an email summary of a portfolio.

Every figure it produces is an illustration, calculated from the funds and allocations you choose.

Where the data comes from

Fund prices and performance come from the Aviva Ireland fund centre, provided by Longboat Analytics and MoneyMate. Fund holdings and asset breakdowns come from the published fund factsheets of Aviva Ireland and Dimensional Fund Advisors. Fund names and trade marks belong to their owners and are used only to identify the funds shown.

Privacy

The tool is a static web page. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own. The figures you type stay in your browser and are not sent anywhere. The page downloads its own fund data files and loads its typeface from Google Fonts.
